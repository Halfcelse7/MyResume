Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information Collection

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The only personal data collected is what you submit through the contact form (name, email, optional website, message), which is processed by Formspree. Fonts and icons are loaded from Google Fonts and cdnjs, which may receive your IP address.

2. Use of Data

Submitted data is used exclusively for professional communication regarding freelance, employment, or project collaboration opportunities.

3. Reference Data Protection

Professional references are not published on this website. Referee contact details are shared privately, on request and with the referee's consent, in line with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).
